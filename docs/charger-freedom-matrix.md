## Interpretation

- **Best first targets:** Alfen, Mennekes Professional, Schneider EVlink Pro AC, KEBA P30, go-e, and exact ABB/Peblar combinations with supported local administration.
- **Cloud-mediated integrations:** Easee, Zaptec, and Smappee may integrate technically but cannot prove survival of that vendor cloud.
- **Do not sell unknowns:** Wallbox, EVBox, Webasto, and all cross-generation families need exact primary evidence and field records.
- **Open hardware:** OpenEVSE/EVerest are allies and reference targets, not evidence that closed installed chargers are migratable.

The versioned evidence dataset—configuration path, credential custody, firmware behavior, buffer limits, metering provenance, recovery, and passed failure tests—is more valuable than the OCPP server.
