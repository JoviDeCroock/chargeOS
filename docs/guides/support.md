# Get help with a company record

Use **Company accounts → Support cases** for a reimbursement or account question. Your company runs this helpdesk. It does not provide emergency assistance or a promised response time. For electrical danger, stop using the equipment and contact the responsible qualified installer or emergency service.

## Open a case

1. Sign in to the correct company workspace.
2. Open your statement and choose **Ask about this statement**, or choose **Support cases → Open a case** for a general question.
3. Choose an issue type, subject and message. Include a session reference if useful. Never paste passwords, AI keys, RFID numbers, bank details or private files.
4. Choose **Save case**. Your organization administrator is assigned first; finance or an administrator can assign another current colleague.

Employees with the driver role see only their own cases. Current company finance and administration colleagues can see company cases and every reply. There are no private staff notes or attachments. Nothing is sent to AI or search. [Your own AI key](ai-help.md) is a separate troubleshooting workflow with its own sharing controls.

## Follow the answer

Open the case to read replies and its assigned person. **Waiting for reporter** means the employee who opened the case needs to reply. Reply and save. **Load earlier replies** and **Load older cases** show retained history.

When the team proposes a resolution, check the answer and linked records. Choose **Confirm the resolution and close**, tick the confirmation and save only when satisfied. Choose **Reopen the case** if more work is needed. Only the reporter confirms closure; a staff answer alone does not close it.

A case never changes an approved amount, makes a payment or changes a charger setting. A correction needs the separate [reimbursement review](reimbursements.md). Check payment through your company's finance process.

## Handle a case as a colleague

Finance and administrators open the company case list and select a case. Under **Next action**, choose **Assign a colleague**, **Ask the reporter for information**, or **Propose a resolution**, complete the fields and choose **Save update**. Only the assigned person or an administrator can request information or propose resolution. Explain the check and outcome. The employee still confirms closure.

## If something goes wrong

If a save result is uncertain, keep the tab open and choose **Retry same update**. Do not open another case to work around it. Reloading, leaving the page or changing devices loses that in-tab retry identifier. If the case changed elsewhere, reload its current history before writing a new update.

A departed assigned colleague needs reassignment by current finance/admin staff. Leaving an organization ends access while preserving history for staff. If the pilot limit is reached, ask your administrator about capacity and retention. chargeOS refuses new content rather than deleting replies.
