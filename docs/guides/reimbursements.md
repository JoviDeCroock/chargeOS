# Review home-charging reimbursements

The current employer workspace calculates and reviews reimbursement records locally. Approval records a financial decision. It does not send a bank transfer, create a tax invoice or mark money paid.

## Prepare the employee's policy

An administrator assigns the employee, vehicle/card aliases and cost center to an immutable policy version for a defined period. The policy specifies the agreed electricity rate. chargeOS does not choose a legally or contractually valid rate for you; the employer supplies the policy they have approved.

## Make a statement

1. Use an authorized session export from the charger or existing provider. Keep the live provider connected while testing imports.
2. Choose the employee's assignment, statement period and exact CSV columns.
3. Create a draft and check included sessions, held records, energy, rate and total. Missing evidence, conflicting sessions or eligibility issues need review.
4. Ask a different authorized finance reviewer to inspect the exact snapshot and approve it with their current authenticator code, or reject it with a reason.
5. The driver can view their own statement and supporting evidence. Finance can inspect the organization's journal and current balance.

Duplicate-session reservations prevent the same sessions being approved twice within an organization. They do not reconcile a payment already made by a different provider. Agree the migration cut-off with the current provider and finance team.

## Correct a mistake

Do not replace an approved snapshot by editing its historical values. Create a linked correction, show the difference and have it independently reviewed. A cancellation also needs a separate reviewer. The journal preserves prior decisions and adjustment receipts.

The current workflow keeps approved records visibly unpaid. Arrange and verify actual payment through your existing finance process; do not tell a driver a transfer happened because a statement was approved.
